The purpose of this study is to construct an LOD model that connects and structures educational materials by utilizing DA resources and a variety of educational information in a machine-readable form. For this purpose, we develop an RDF dataset and a SPARQL endpoint using data from the S×UKILAM archive. As a result, we are able to construct a findable LOD model that connects educational materials with diverse information such as Courses of Study codes, Japan Search and NHK video contents in a highly machine-readable form.
