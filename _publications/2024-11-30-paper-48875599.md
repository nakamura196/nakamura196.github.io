Dai Nihon Shiryo, a comprehensive index of Japanese historical materials, has been printed in paper format for 125 years. This paper outlines the process and challenges involved in converting these materials to comply with the TEI P5 Guidelines, focusing on the Sixth Series (Nanboku-cho period) as a sample for defining the basic structure of previously published content. It specifically examines the structuring of Koubun (event data), text reuse from diverse materials, and annotations.
