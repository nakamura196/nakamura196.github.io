Although the digitization of modern photographic material has been acerating, it is essential for the effective use of these materials to digitize the semantic information such as the location where the picture was taken and the name of the person photographed. In this study, we have developed “Shibusawa Eiichi Photograph”, a crowd-annotation system that aims to annotate over 700 pictures included in the supplementary volume 10 of Shibusawa Eiichi Denki Shiryo. This system supports the tagging of people in photographs, identification of locations where photographs were taken, transcription of textual information, uploading of related photographs, and registration of bibliographic information.
