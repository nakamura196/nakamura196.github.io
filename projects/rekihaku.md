## Role

Structured the *Engishiki* text in TEI and built its viewer with IIIF images. Built nihuBridge LOD from Rekihaku's databases "Nihon Shōen" and the bibliography of shōen studies; co-authors used it to design and test a high-school learning model.

## Outputs

- [Digital *Engishiki*](https://khirin-t.rekihaku.ac.jp/engishiki/) 2022-04
- [nihuBridge LOD — Shōen Database](https://shoen.vercel.app) 2024-09
