## Role

Technical lead of the project from its design stage (talk on the planned archive system, 2018-02). Designed and built the archive platform (Omeka-based collections, IIIF, TEI, LOD) and the cross-departmental UTokyo Digital Archive Portal. Worked on data preservation (2021) and presented the project at more than ten meetings from 2018 to 2021. Continues to operate the platform and portal as a member of the team.
