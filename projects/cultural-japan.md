## Role

Developed the search system, the IIIF viewers and IIIF Discovery, as listed on the team page. Now runs the whole service, including operation and maintenance, except for the RDF store. As of 2026-11, Cultural Japan holds 2,778,052 records from 204 databases, including Japan Search and overseas museums such as the British Museum and the Art Institute of Chicago.
