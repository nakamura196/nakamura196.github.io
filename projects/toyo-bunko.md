## Role

Built the group's research databases and the IIIF image repository, working with Toyo Bunko researchers on the Buddhist canon and map materials. Applies multilingual OCR, TEI, IIIF and generative AI to the Toyo Bunko's East Asian materials (talk, 2026-08). Co-investigator on the two KAKENHI projects on the Chinese Buddhist canon.

## Outputs

- [Original and Collated Texts Database of the *Taishō Shinshū Daizōkyō*](https://static.toyobunko-lab.jp/taishozo/) 2021-07
- [Catalogue Database of the Jiaxing Canon held by Yurenja (formerly Zōjōji Hōonzō)](https://static.toyobunko-lab.jp/u-renja/) 2021-07
- [Toyo Bunko *Shuijingzhu* Maps Database](https://static.toyobunko-lab.jp/suikeichuzu/) 2021-11

- [Toyo Bunko Chinese Classics Database (about 100,000 records)](https://kanseki.toyobunko-lab.jp/) 2026-10
