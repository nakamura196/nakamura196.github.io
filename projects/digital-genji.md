## Role

Developer of the system and first author of its papers. Built the text-research platform with IIIF and TEI, and the AI image search that compares manuscripts and printed editions using kuzushiji OCR and edit distance. Worked with Takashi Tamura, Kiyonori Nagasaki and the University of Tokyo Library staff study group ("Ura-Genji").
