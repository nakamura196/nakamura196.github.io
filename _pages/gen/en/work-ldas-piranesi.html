Description / abstract
A database of the 1,440 original etchings in the 29 volumes of the Opere di Giovanni Battista Piranesi (Paris, 1835–39) in the General Library's Kamei Collection, rebuilt from a database made under a COE research grant. Browse by volume and page, or search by keyword, subject or the place in Rome on a map.
