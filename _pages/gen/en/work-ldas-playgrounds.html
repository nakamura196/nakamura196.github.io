Description / abstract
A record of playgrounds in Vancouver's parks, made searchable by type of play equipment. Parks from the City's open data are shown on a map and in a list; photos taken on site are kept on Wikimedia Commons and shown through IIIF. The equipment vocabulary is published in SKOS.
