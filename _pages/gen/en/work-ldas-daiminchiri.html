Description / abstract
A digital archive of the Daimin Chiri no Zu, an Edo-period manuscript in the Toyo Bunko that maps Ming China in four scrolls. The images carry 3,897 annotations of place names and other writing, which can be searched in detail, located on a present-day map and downloaded as a dataset.
