Description / abstract
Fifteen vellum contract documents written in Fes, Morocco, between the 16th and 19th centuries, with edited Arabic texts, English descriptions and ultraviolet photographs linked together. Browse by document or search individual deeds, and read the text against the image. TEI/XML, DTS and IIIF.
